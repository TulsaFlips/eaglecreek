Privacy Policy

Eagle Creek · last updated October 20, 2026

Who uses it

Eagle Creek is private software used only by Eagle Creek Holdings LLC and its owner. There are no other users and no accounts for the public.

What it accesses

  • QuickBooks Online: the company's own accounts, vendors, customers, transactions and reports, read to bring the company's QuickBooks history into Eagle Creek's books and to compare the two. Nothing is sent to QuickBooks or changed there.
  • Bank and card activity (through Plaid), when the owner connects an account.
  • Emails the owner chooses to save from Gmail.

Where it's kept

On the owner's own devices and in the owner's own private Supabase database. Sign-in tokens for QuickBooks and Plaid are kept in the Mac's keychain.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with or shown to anyone else. There's no advertising and no tracking. Data goes only between the owner's devices, the owner's Supabase project and the services the owner connects (QuickBooks, Plaid, Gmail).

Removing data

Disconnecting QuickBooks or a bank in Eagle Creek's settings stops any further access. The owner can delete the data from their devices and their Supabase project at any time.
