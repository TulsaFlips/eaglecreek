# Eagle Creek for phone, iPad and Chrome

The job-site side of Eagle Creek, in the Mac app's colours and layout: projects, progress
photos, daily logs, punch lists, schedules, documents and the calendar. It uses the same
Supabase account as the Mac app, so anything done on the phone shows up on the Mac at its next
sync (every few minutes, or **Sync Now**), and the other way round.

## What it does

- **Projects:** grouped like the Mac's sidebar (Active Projects, Pipeline, Rentals, Closed), with
  status colours, address and directions, schedule progress, late tasks and open punch items.
  Inside a project, the Mac's sections: Overview, Contract on client jobs (Terms, Schedule of
  Values, Change Orders), Budget (Budget, Selections, Subs & POs),
  Schedule (Schedule, Daily Logs, Punch List), Money (Summary, Draws or Pay Applications,
  Transactions, Lien Waivers, Reports) and Files (Photos, Documents). Each section remembers the page you were
  last on.
- **Budget:** every line by category with its quantity and cost, what's been billed against it in
  the books, % done and the typed actual, with the contingency, total, per square foot, billed to
  date and budget left worked out exactly as the Mac does. Lines billed over budget are flagged.
  Tap a line for what's been paid and the bills behind it. The budget itself is changed on the Mac.
  A flip still on a quick rehab estimate (set on the Mac's Purchase & Sale page) shows the
  estimate as its budget, with the lines so far underneath; its draws and job cost say so too.
- **Selections:** the finish schedule: every finish and fixture by category, with product, color,
  quantity, cost, supplier and status (TBD, GC Choice, Locked, Ordered, Installed, N/A). Order-by
  dates come from the schedule (the task it's needed for less the lead time) or a firm date, and
  items late or due to order this week are called out. On client jobs, allowances and overages
  at the job's markup. Add, change and delete selections, start from the standard list or one of
  your finish packages. Marking one Ordered stamps today's order date, as on the Mac.
- **Subs & POs:** each subcontract and purchase order with what's committed, billed and left, and
  the retainage held.
- **Books:** the Mac's Books screen. The overview has cash in the bank, bills to pay (overdue in
  red), what you're owed, profit this year, money in and out for six months, each bank and card
  balance, bills due and lien waivers to collect. Then every transaction (search, filter by
  type, status and dates), receipts waiting to be entered on the Mac, lien waivers, the chart of
  accounts with each account's activity and running balance, and reports: Profit & Loss (for
  the company, its overhead or one project), Profit by Project, Balance Sheet, Bills to Pay and
  Owed to You by age, Vendor Payments & 1099s, and Money In and Out. Every number comes from the
  Mac's own bookkeeping rules, so it matches the Mac to the cent.
- **A project's Money:** the summary (spent to date, unpaid bills, draws received or what the
  client owes, waivers needed, the deal's budget vs actual by cost group or a client job's
  contract vs cost, costs by account, recent activity), the draw schedule with what the lender
  has funded and what's been billed and paid against each draw, its transactions and lien
  waivers, and its reports (Job Cost, Draw Reconciliation, P&L, bills and 1099s).
- **Contracts (client jobs):** the terms (client, type, retainage, payment terms, signed, start
  and completion dates against where the schedule finishes), the schedule of values with what's
  been billed on each line, and change orders. Write a change order on site (what changes, your
  cost, the price at the job's markup, days added), mark it sent or rejected, and attach the
  signed copy; approve it on the Mac, since approving changes the contract and the budget.
  Selections over the client's allowances can go straight onto a change order.
- **Pay applications:** each one worked out like the Mac's (contract sum, completed and stored,
  retainage, previous applications, deposit, current payment due, balance to finish) with its
  continuation sheet line by line, and its invoice's status. Cost-plus and T&M jobs show their
  billing instead: billed, earned, cost to date and invoices.
- **From the site:** collect a lien waiver (snap the signed copy; it's attached to the payment
  and marked received, progress or final), mark a draw Requested or Inspected with the
  inspector's notes, and add progress photos or paperwork to a draw for the lender. Bills,
  invoices and deposits are still entered on the Mac.
- **Photos:** take or pick photos straight into a project's Progress Photos, or into a daily log.
  They're resized on the phone (2400 px) so they upload fast from the site. Swipe between
  photos; delete one taken by mistake. The project's photo folders (Demo, Framing…) are along
  the top with how many photos each holds: pick one to see its photos, and photos you take or
  pick then go into it. **New Folder** adds one, and the folder button in the photo viewer moves
  a photo to another folder. Folders are renamed and deleted on the Mac.
- **Notes:** your own notes and checklists, pinned, in folders, on a project when they belong
  to one, dictated or typed, on every device (see Notes, below).
- **Daily Logs:** today's log in one tap, with the last log's crews carried over. Same sections
  as the Mac (Weather, Crews on Site, Work Performed, Deliveries and Inspections, Problems,
  Delays and Safety, Notes), weather filled in for the project's city, photos attached. Saves
  as you type. **Voice Note** opens today's log and takes down what you say (see Voice notes).
- **Punch List:** add items; the square marks them done, as on the Mac. Ready to check is in each
  item's details.
- **Schedule:** the Mac's dates (workdays, US holidays, tasks that wait on others). Set Not
  Started, Working On It, Stuck or Done, percent complete and notes. The **Schedule** tab shows
  the next two weeks across every active project, stuck and late work first.
- **Documents:** every file on a project by type. Share / Save keeps a copy.
- **Plan reader and takeoffs:** any PDF opens as a drawing set (the **Plans** button on a
  project's overview goes straight to its Plans & Specs). See below.
- **Calendar:** day and week views of your events and active projects' tasks. Add, edit and
  delete events, with the Mac's options (repeat, reminder, project, who it's with, delete only
  one repeat).
- **Contacts:** every trade, supplier, crew member, customer and lender. Call, text or email in a
  tap; see their projects, open tasks and punch items, subcontracts and POs, bills and invoices
  (what you've paid them this year, what's owed, lien waivers still needed), and every file tied
  to them: W-9s, insurance certificates, quotes, invoices, waivers and subcontract paperwork,
  matched the same way as on the Mac. Expired insurance and missing W-9s are flagged. Add or edit
  contacts and add files to them (a W-9 marks it on file; an insurance certificate can update the
  expiry date). Bills, invoices and subcontracts are view-only here.
- **Trips & Mileage:** start a trip (destination, dates, the business purpose the IRS wants written
  down, which projects it's for) and add every cost as you go: snap the receipt, pick what it was
  (meals, lodging, airfare, car rental, fuel, parking), and it's filed the way the Mac files it
  (travel to Travel – Air, Lodging & Car; meals to Business Meals, usually 50% deductible). Each
  trip shows its total, meals, mileage, what you paid yourself and which expenses still need a
  receipt, plus boarding passes, folios and rental agreements. It all syncs to the Mac's Trips &
  Mileage, where the Trip Report for your accountant is made.
- **Canadian dollars:** enter a receipt in CAD and it's turned into US dollars for the books at that
  day's Bank of Canada rate (the European Central Bank's, through Frankfurter, if the Bank of
  Canada can't be reached; or type the rate from your card statement). The original CA$ amount,
  the rate and where it came from are kept on the expense.
- **Paid with your own card or cash:** every purchase asks who paid. The company's cards and
  accounts, or your own money, either **owed back** (Owed to Owner, as on the Mac) or **kept in as
  your contribution** (a journal entry from Owed to Owner to Owner Contributions, so nothing is
  owed back). Switch any personal purchase between the two later, or turn part of what the company
  owes you into a contribution in one go (Trips › Paid Personally).
- **Mileage in miles (or kilometres):** log each drive in miles by default; the switch on the
  Mileage page changes to km on that device. Odometer start shows, in grey, where the last drive
  in that vehicle ended: the highest reading on the latest day (a hint only; nothing is filled in). Stored in miles for the Mac, at the IRS
  rate per mile, then posted to the books as owed back or as your contribution.
- **Days in the US:** counted from trips (any part of a day counts) and any other stays you add,
  with this year's count, the last 365 days, and the weighted three-year total the IRS uses for
  its substantial presence test. It's a count, not tax advice; your cross-border accountant can
  tell you what applies.
- **Receipts:** the receipt button on Projects sends a photo to Books › Receipts on the Mac.
- **Appearance:** Light (the default, like the Mac), Dark or Match Device, in Settings (the gear
  on Projects on a phone; the bottom of the sidebar on an iPad or computer).
- **Icons** in the same line style as the Mac's: on section pills, card titles and dashboard
  figures, by trade (plumbing, electrical, roofing…) on budget lines, selections, tasks and trades,
  by kind on documents, accounts and travel expenses, and by weather on daily logs.
- **Emails:** label an email in Gmail, or forward it, and it's saved to a project's Files › Emails
  or to the business Email Archive as a PDF of the conversation, with its attachments beside it
  (see Save emails from Gmail below).
- **Works offline.** Everything is saved on the device first and uploads when there's signal.

Entering bills, invoices and deposits (travel expenses and personal purchases can be entered
here), the budget lines themselves, contract terms and the schedule of values, approving change
orders, making pay applications, QuickBooks, bank imports and reconciling stay on the Mac.

## With Eagle Creek v1.3 and Eagle Creek Books

The web app works with Eagle Creek v1.3 and later and with Eagle Creek Books (the Mac app
for bank feeds, reconciling, 1099s and the accountant package). All three use the same
records in your Supabase project.

- **Nothing is overwritten by an older copy.** v1.3's `setup.sql` makes Supabase keep the newer
  copy when two devices save the same record. The web app checks what Supabase actually took:
  - Changes kept as steps (selections, draws, lien waivers, change orders, trips, expenses) are
    made again on the newer copy and sent, so both edits are kept.
  - Any other change gives way to the newer one, as on the Mac, and the app says so.
  - An edit here is always stamped after the copy it was made on, so a Mac whose clock runs fast
    can't make it look older.
- **Tags** from Eagle Creek Books show on each entry in Books › Transactions. Filter by a tag, or
  search for one.
- **Money recorded in both sets of books** (Eagle Creek Books' Business ↔ Personal) is marked
  on the entry. Changing its amount or how it was paid, or deleting it, reminds you to change
  the personal side in Eagle Creek Books too.
- **Personal card lines in Eagle Creek Books.** A purchase entered here as paid personally is
  offered under **Already in the business books** when its card line reaches Eagle Creek Books
  (v1.3.1). Only the personal side is added there, so it isn't counted twice.
  - Kept in as your contribution: the personal side goes to Investment in the Business.
  - Canadian-dollar lines are found by the CA$ amount kept on the expense.
- **W-9 details** (tax classification, the last four of the taxpayer ID, the 1099 mailing
  address) show on the vendor's contact page. They're entered in Eagle Creek Books. Editing a
  contact here keeps them.
- **Vendor credits, credit memos and refunds** (Eagle Creek v1.4.2) show in Books ›
  Transactions (filter **Credits & refunds**) with what's left of each credit, where it went and
  its refunds. They're made and applied on the Mac; here they're view-only, and a bill or invoice
  shows the credits taken off it. Unused credits count against what's owed on the aging reports
  and the contact's page, a vendor credit comes off its budget line's costs, and a credit taken
  off a bill isn't counted as paid on the 1099 report or for lien waivers.
- **The change history** in Eagle Creek Books shows changes made here as **Web app**.
- **On a Mac,** in Chrome or Safari, an entry's sheet has **Open in Eagle Creek** and **Open in
  Eagle Creek Books**, and Books › Overview links to Eagle Creek Books' Banking and 1099s.

## Save emails from Gmail

Keep the emails that matter with the job they're about (the client picking tile, the city
approving a permit, a sub's quote) and the business ones in an archive of their own (the bank,
the accountant, insurance, the state).

- **Label it in Gmail** (the phone app or the website): **Eagle Creek/Business**, or
  **Eagle Creek/** and the project's name. The project labels are made for you and kept up to
  date as projects open and close.
- **Or forward it** from any other inbox to your Gmail address with a tag:
  `you+business@gmail.com`, `you+104@gmail.com` (the job number) or `you+77birchflip@gmail.com`
  (the project's name, no spaces). Each project's Files › Emails page shows its label and address,
  with a button to copy the address. The saved email is credited to whoever wrote the original.
- Within about five minutes it's saved as a PDF of the whole conversation (who, when, every
  message). Each attachment is saved as its own file beside it, with a type guessed from its name
  the same way the Mac does (an attached permit lands in Permits & Inspections). Signature logos
  and calendar invites are skipped. In Gmail the email moves to **Eagle Creek/Saved**.
- **Where they show:** a project's **Files › Emails**; the **Email Archive** (the envelope on
  Projects on a phone, **Email** in the sidebar on an iPad or computer) with **Business** and
  **All Emails**, searchable by subject, sender, project or the words in the latest message; and
  the contact's page, when the sender's email address matches a contact.
- **An email** opens to who it's from and to, the date, its attachments, the latest message, and
  buttons to open the PDF or the conversation in Gmail. **Move to** files it in another project
  or the business archive; its attachments go with it.
- On the Mac (v1.3.2 and later) emails are their own type, **Emails**, with a folder in each
  project's Finder folder. An older Mac shows them as Other; their Email tag keeps them emails here.

**Setting it up** (once, about 10 minutes, on a computer):

1. In this app, **Settings › Save Emails from Gmail › Copy Setup Script**. It copies a short
   script with your Supabase address, key and sign-in email filled in (`gmail-bridge.gs`).
2. Go to [script.google.com](https://script.google.com), signed in to the business Gmail. Make
   a new project, paste over what's there, and save.
3. **Project Settings** (the gear) › **Script Properties** › add `EC_PASSWORD` with your Eagle
   Creek password. The password lives only there, not in the script.
4. Back in the editor, choose **setup** in the menu at the top and click **Run**. Google asks for
   permission: Review permissions › your account › Advanced › Go to the project › Allow ("unsafe"
   only means Google hasn't reviewed a script you added for yourself). The log says what it did.

From then on it checks Gmail every 5 minutes. Settings shows which Gmail it's using, when it last
checked, how many it has saved, and anything it couldn't save (those get the Gmail label
**Eagle Creek/Not Saved**; label them again to retry). To stop it, run **stop** in the script.

How it works: the script runs in your own Google account. It signs in to Supabase as you, with
the publishable (or anon) key, and refuses the secret key. Your emails go from Gmail straight
to your own Supabase project, as ordinary files: the conversation is a file of type `email`
tagged `Email`, and each attachment points back to it. Saving the same email again updates the
same file. The script also keeps a small status record (`mailBridge`) with the labels and
addresses it uses.

## The bank feed on your phone

Eagle Creek Books (Mac v1.3.6 and later) keeps the bank and card lines it brings in from Plaid or
statement files in its own Supabase table (`ledger_documents`, from `supabase/setup.sql` v1.3).
**Books › Bank** here shows the business's lines waiting For Review. Tap one to:

- **Match** the entry already in the books (the ones for the same amount within a few days are
  offered; the entry is marked cleared, like Books does).
- **Add** it as an expense or deposit, with a vendor, category and project; the category and
  project are filled in from the last time that payee was recorded. Splits across projects and
  transfers between accounts are done in Books.
- **Leave Out** a personal charge or a duplicate.

Decided lines show under Decided This Week with **Undo**. The phone uses the Mac's ids and fields,
so Books shows each decision as its own and nothing is doubled; its Undo works on them too.

## Flips and Work in Progress

Eagle Creek Books (Mac v1.3.4 and later) keeps a flip's costs in **Work in Progress – Properties**
until the property sells, then moves them to **Cost of Properties Sold** on the sale date, with one
journal entry a month and one on the sale date. Here, those entries count in the books (balances
and the P&L), but a project's cost pages count each bill and expense once, in its own category,
so budget vs actual and costs by category read the same as before.

## Plan reader

- **Move around:** pinch to zoom, drag to move, double-tap to zoom in (again to zoom out).
  On a computer, pinch the trackpad or hold Ctrl while scrolling. Whatever is on screen is redrawn
  sharp, so small dimensions stay readable. **Fit** shows the whole sheet; the arrows (or the
  sheet name) change sheets.
- **Set the scale** for each sheet (**Scale** at the bottom), once:
  - **Measure a Known Dimension** (most reliable): tap both ends of a dimension printed on the
    drawing and type its length.
  - Or pick the **printed scale** (1/4" = 1'-0", 1" = 20' and so on). This is only right when
    the PDF is full size, not a half-size 11×17 set.
  - Either can be used for every sheet in the set.
- **Measure:** **Length** (two taps), **Run** (tap point to point for walls, trim, pipe; Done to
  finish), **Area** (tap each corner; tap the first corner or Done to close; shows sq ft and the
  distance around), **Count** (tap each outlet, fixture or post). A magnifier shows under your
  finger while you place a point, and the **Straight** button keeps lines level, plumb or at 45°.
- **Change one:** tap it in Move to name it ("Kitchen floor"), change its colour, drag its white
  dots, or delete it.
- **The list** (top right) totals every sheet and copies everything as text for an estimate or
  a message.
- Scales and measurements sync between your phones and iPads. The Mac app doesn't show them
  yet; they're saved so it can later.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (one time, about 10 minutes, iPad or computer)

All the app's files sit in one folder with no subfolders, so it uploads from an iPad too.

1. Unzip `EagleCreek-Web.zip`. On an iPad, tap it in the Files app; on a Mac, double-click it.
2. Sign in at **github.com** (in Safari on an iPad), tap **+** (top right) → **New repository**.
   Name it `eaglecreek`, choose **Public**, tap **Create repository**.
3. Tap **uploading an existing file**, then **choose your files**. In the `eaglecreek-web`
   folder, tap **Select**, then select all the files (or drag them all in on a Mac).
   Tap **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under **Branch**, pick **main** and **/ (root)**, tap **Save**.
5. Wait a minute or two and refresh. Your address appears at the top:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/eaglecreek/`

To update later: open the repository, **Add file → Upload files**, add all the files from the
new `eaglecreek-web` folder and commit. Phones pick up the new version the next time the app
is opened (never while you're typing).

Public only means the app's code can be seen. Your data never goes to GitHub: the page talks
straight to your Supabase project, and only your email and password can open it.

## Each phone or iPad

1. **iPhone / iPad:** open your address in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then open
   Eagle Creek from the Home Screen. Do this before signing in: the Home Screen app keeps its
   own sign-in, separate from Safari.
   **Android:** open it in Chrome, then ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
   **Computer:** in Chrome, the install icon in the address bar.
2. Sign in with the Supabase project URL and anon (or publishable) key, and the email and
   password you sync with on the Mac. The URL and key are in the Mac app under Settings →
   Cloud Sync, or on supabase.com under your project's **Connect** button (or Project Settings →
   API Keys). Never use the secret or service_role key.
3. Once one device is set up, **Settings → Set Up Another Device** makes a link with the URL and
   key. On the next device, paste it into the first box on the sign-in screen; both boxes fill in.

The Mac app needs Cloud Sync turned on with the same account. If it isn't yet, set it up in the
Mac app's Settings → Cloud Sync and run `supabase/setup.sql` in the Supabase SQL Editor.

## Notes (1.18.0)

A notes app of its own, in the **Notes** tab, so the phone's notes app can stay closed:

- **Notes and checklists.** A note is a title and as much text as you like; a checklist is a
  list of items to tick off (Enter adds the next item, Backspace on an empty one removes it,
  **Remove Done** clears the ticked ones). Either turns into the other from the ⋯ menu.
- **Pinned** notes sit on top. The rest are grouped by when they were last edited (Today,
  Yesterday, Previous 7 Days, Previous 30 Days, then by month).
- **Folders** are just names: move a note to one, or type a new one. The chips at the top show
  one folder, Pinned, or everything.
- **A project on a note** when it belongs to one, so searching the project's name finds it.
- **Search** looks through titles, text, checklist items, folders and project names as you type.
- **Voice notes:** the mic at the top starts listening at once and puts what you say into a new
  note; the mic in any note adds to it (see Voice notes, below).
- **Share, copy, duplicate, delete** from the ⋯ menu. Share hands the note to Messages, Mail or
  anything else on the phone.
- Saves as you type, works with no signal, and syncs through the same account as everything
  else, so the same notes are on your phone, iPad and computer (open the app in Chrome on the
  computer, where it has the sidebar). Notes are the owner's; team members don't see the tab.

## Voice notes (1.17.0)

Every notes box (a daily log's Work Performed, Deliveries, Inspections, Visitors, Problems,
Safety and Notes, a task's notes, an event's notes) has a mic. Tap it, talk, tap it again:
the words go into the box as they're recognised and save like typing would, after whatever
was already there. On a project's Daily Logs page, **Voice Note** starts listening at once
and opens today's log (starting one if there isn't one), with the words landing in Notes.

- The first time, the phone asks to allow the microphone for the app.
- The words come from the phone's own speech recognition (Apple's on iPhone and iPad, Google's
  in Chrome), so it needs a connection. Apple's puts in punctuation on its own; "new line",
  "new paragraph", "full stop" and "question mark" also work.
- On an Android phone the recognizer works a phrase at a time: there's a short tone between
  phrases, and the app keeps listening on its own until you tap the mic.
- Listening stops on its own after a long pause (iPhone); tap the mic to carry on.
- If the mic on the page says dictation isn't available (iOS doesn't give it to every
  home-screen install), tap into the box and use the keyboard's own mic key, which always
  works.

## The team (1.14.0)

People the owner adds in Eagle Creek on the Mac (Settings › Team) sign in here with a **username and password**. Each sees only what the owner allows (None, View or Edit for Projects & Schedule, Files & Photos, Contacts, Budgets & Contracts, Timesheets, Books & Banking, Trips & Mileage). Tabs they can't see are gone, pages outside their access say so, and a change they can't make is stopped with a note. The database enforces all of it (Eagle Creek's `supabase/setup.sql`), so it holds whatever the phone does. There's no way to make an account here.

- **Signing in.** Type a username (it signs in as `<username>@team.eaglecreek.invalid`, an address that never gets mail) or an email.
- **Built-in project (1.14.1).** The project URL and publishable key are in `config.js`, so the sign-in screen asks only for a username (or email) and password; "Use a different Supabase project" under the button is still there. Blank them out and it asks for the URL and key (or a setup link) as before.
- **Settings** shows who's signed in, their role and, for a member, what they may see and change.
- **Access changed or turned off.** Records of an area taken away leave the phone on the next sync; an area given back comes back. Turned off by the owner, the phone erases its copy and can't sign in again.
- **Files** live in the owner's folder, whoever adds them.
- Works with a Supabase project that hasn't set up the team yet (everything, as before).

## Approving Claude (connect.html)

`connect.html` is where you approve the Claude connector (Eagle Creek's `supabase/functions/eaglecreek-mcp`; the Mac README › The Claude connector has the setup). When you click Connect in Claude, Supabase sends you here (Authentication › OAuth Server › Authorization Path = `/connect.html`). Sign in as Claude's own team member (`claude`), check the access it lists, and click Allow or Deny.

- Only an enabled team member can approve; the owner's own sign-in is refused before the request is even read, so Claude never works as the owner.
- The sign-in lives only in the page's memory and is signed out (that session only) once the answer is sent. Nothing is stored in the browser.
- It warns when the app asking doesn't return to claude.ai, shows names as plain text, and won't work inside another page.

## The lock (1.13.0)

Right after signing in, the app asks for a **six-digit PIN**, and offers **Face ID / Touch ID**
where the device can give the app a key from a passkey (iOS 18 Safari does; where it can't, the
PIN is used and the app says so). Everything the app keeps on the device (your sign-in, every
record, every photo and document copy) is encrypted with a key that exists only while the app is
unlocked; the PIN (PBKDF2, 310,000 rounds) or your face unwraps it. So a phone taken while locked,
or its browser storage copied off it, gives up nothing readable.

- The app locks when it has been in the background for **2 minutes** (Settings › Security: right
  away, 1, 2, 5 or 15 minutes), and **Lock Now** is there too.
- Five wrong PINs bring a 30-second wait that doubles each time; **ten wrong PINs erase the
  device's copy**, as does **two weeks without unlocking**. Your books stay in the cloud: sign in
  again and set a new PIN.
- **Signed out from the Mac** (Eagle Creek Books › Settings › Security): the phone erases its copy
  the next time it reaches the server and shows the sign-in screen with a note saying why.
  **Sign Out All Other Devices** there also ends the phone's session at Supabase at once.
- Every device registers itself (name, app version, whether the lock is on) so the Mac can list
  them. Settings › Security on the phone shows this device's name.
- The page carries a content security policy: it can only talk to its own files, your Supabase
  project and the weather and exchange-rate services.

## Good to know

- Edits follow the Mac's rule: the newest edit wins. If the same punch item is changed on the
  phone and the Mac between syncs, the later change is kept (and the app says when the Mac's won).
- Selections, draws and photo folders are saved inside the project, lien waivers on the bill, change orders and
  trips on their own, all of which the Mac also edits. Each change made here is kept as a step until it reaches Supabase; if the
  record changed on the Mac in the meantime (even while the phone had no signal), the step is
  made again on the Mac's newer copy, so neither edit is lost. The one case to avoid: changing
  the same project on the Mac in the few minutes before it syncs, while selections are changed
  here. Then the Mac keeps both versions (one as a conflict copy), as it does between two Macs.
- The pill at the top shows sync. Tap it to sync now (or, if there's a problem, to see why).
  Photos waiting to upload say so on the photo.
- In the closed books (set on the Mac), an expense's memo and trip can still change here; its
  amount, date, category and how it was paid can't, and nothing moves in or out of them.
- An expense reconciled in Eagle Creek Books (Mac v1.3.5 and later) keeps its date, account and
  amount here too, and can't be deleted; its category, memo and trip can still change.
- Receipts wait in Books › Receipts on the Mac. **Enter as Expense** there reads the store,
  date and total.
- Reminders on events come from the Mac app's notifications.
- **Settings → Free Up Space** removes downloaded copies of photos and documents; they download
  again when opened. Signing out removes everything on that device, and warns first if
  anything hasn't uploaded yet.
- Files over 50 MB can't sync (Supabase's limit), same as on the Mac.
- PDFs are shown with Mozilla's pdf.js (`pdf.min.js` and `pdf.worker.min.js`, Apache 2.0 licence; see `PDFJS-LICENSE.txt`).
