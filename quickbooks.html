Eagle Creek and QuickBooks

A private app for Eagle Creek Holdings LLC

Connecting

Eagle Creek connects to QuickBooks Online from the Mac app: Settings › QuickBooks › Sign In to QuickBooks…

Disconnected?

If the connection was ended from QuickBooks, Eagle Creek stops sending. To connect again, open Eagle Creek on the Mac and click Sign In Again in Settings › QuickBooks.
